* Why are we talking about this?

** Engagement 102

Poeple do not leave companies. They leave managers.

**Poeple want to work for something (a mission/purpose) or somebody.**

** The Cathedral

#+REVEAL_HTML: <img src="./images/camelot.jpeg" width="30%"/>

As leaders, we need to be able to answer two questions:

- What are we building/doing?
- Why does what we do matter?

**Let's build a cathedral!**

* Reasons why people work

** Weak reasons

- Money
- Work-life balance
- Having fun
- Learning something useful
- Waiting for the exit or the next better offer

** Strong Reasons

- I believe in the purpose of the work
- I can explain why it matters to me
- I am growing by doing something difficult
- I get to work with people whose example teaches me something

** Something or Someone

You either work for **something** or **someone**.

** Something or Someone

You either **care** for **something** or **someone**.

* How to make people care?

** Ask Why!

Once a month, ask:

- Why do you do what you do?
- Why does it matter?

Possible answers:

- I do not know
- Because you told me to do it
- Because this matters to me

** Connect the dots

#+REVEAL_HTML: <img src="./images/cathedral.jpeg" width="50%"/>

Be prepared to explain:

- how the cathedral we are building looks like
- why this person's work matters
- why your work matters
- how their work helps other people succeed

** Rinse and repeat

- Ask why
- Listen carefully
- Connect the dots
- Repeat every month

* More Thoughts

** Care comes in two shapes (I)

Care for:

- one person
- a group of people
- a team you lead
- a team you belong to

** Care comes in two shapes (II)

Care about:

- a big idea/a small task
- a value/believe (e.g. trust, respect, camaraderie, ...)  

** Choose

**You need to choose who to care about and what to care about!**

Nobody else can do this for you!

** Belonging

Care creates belonging. Everyone wants to belong.

(Don't think that you do not want/need to belong)
