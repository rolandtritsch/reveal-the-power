* Why This Matters

** Engagement 102

#+REVEAL_HTML: <img src="./images/camelot.jpeg" width="30%"/>

People do not stay committed because a company has a good slogan.

They stay committed when they can connect their work to something they care about.

#+BEGIN_NOTES
The original deck framed this as "how to make people stay." The stronger framing is about helping people decide what is worth caring about.
#+END_NOTES

** The Leadership Question

As leaders, we need to be able to answer two questions:

- Why does this work matter?
- Why should the person in front of me care?

#+BEGIN_NOTES
The second question is the one leaders skip most often. Generic purpose does not create commitment.
#+END_NOTES

* Reasons People Stay

** Weak Reasons

- More money than somewhere else
- Work-life balance
- Having fun
- Learning something useful
- Waiting for the exit or the next better offer

#+REVEAL_HTML: <p style="font-size: 1.3em;"><strong>These reasons are weak because they are easy to copy.</strong></p>

#+BEGIN_NOTES
These are not bad reasons. They matter. But they are not differentiated, and they rarely create durable commitment by themselves.
#+END_NOTES

** Strong Reasons

- I believe in the purpose of the work
- I can explain why it matters to me
- I am growing by doing something difficult
- I get to work with people whose example teaches me

#+BEGIN_NOTES
This is the move from perks and conditions to meaning and growth.
#+END_NOTES

* Make People Care

** Ask Why

Once a month, ask:

#+REVEAL_HTML: <p style="font-size: 1.3em;"><strong>Why do you do what you do?</strong></p>
#+REVEAL_HTML: <p style="font-size: 1.3em;"><strong>Why does it matter?</strong></p>

Possible answers:

- I do not know
- Because you told me to do it
- Because this matters to me

#+BEGIN_NOTES
The first two answers are signals. They tell you where leadership work is needed.
#+END_NOTES

** Building Cathedrals

#+REVEAL_HTML: <img src="./images/cathedral.jpeg" width="50%"/>

#+BEGIN_NOTES
The cathedral story is useful because it separates the task from the meaning of the task.
#+END_NOTES

** Connect The Dots

Be prepared to explain:

- the cathedral we are building
- why this person's work matters
- why your work matters
- how their work helps other people succeed

#+BEGIN_NOTES
Do not make people infer the connection. Say it clearly, repeatedly, and personally.
#+END_NOTES

** Repeat The Practice

- Ask why
- Listen carefully
- Connect the dots
- Repeat every month
- Care enough to notice when the answer changes

* More Thoughts

** Care Has Shape

Care for:

- a group of people
- one person
- a team you lead
- a team you belong to

Care about:

- a big idea
- a small task
- a responsibility nobody else sees

** Choose Deliberately

#+REVEAL_HTML: <p style="font-size: 1.5em;"><strong>You need to choose what to care for and what to care about.</strong></p>

If you never choose, the work will choose for you.

#+BEGIN_NOTES
The old version said, "Otherwise you will be unhappy." Keep the point, but make it more precise.
#+END_NOTES

** Belonging

#+REVEAL_HTML: <p style="font-size: 1.5em;">Care creates belonging.</p>

Everyone wants to belong to something or somebody.

Even the most independent people should not fight that too hard.
